   - Articles discussing the weaponization of GitLab email addresses and the exploitation of npm packages highlight the increasing focus on supply chain vulnerabilities. This trend underscores the necessity for organizations to adopt a more holistic view of cybersecurity that includes third-party risk management.

The cybersecurity landscape is evolving rapidly, with AI playing a dual role as both a tool for attackers and defenders. Collaboration across business functions and a focus on supply chain security will be crucial in building resilient cybersecurity strategies.
