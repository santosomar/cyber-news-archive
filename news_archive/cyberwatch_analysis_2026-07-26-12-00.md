The emergence of the DevMan RaaS portal illustrates the growing trend of organized cybercrime, where services are commoditized and made accessible to less skilled attackers. The Rockwell article highlights ongoing vulnerabilities in industrial control systems (ICS). As industries become more digitized, the security of these systems is paramount. As AI technologies become more integrated into various applications, the risk of exploitation increases. This trend necessitates a focus on AI security, ensuring that AI models are not only effective but also secure against adversarial attacks.
